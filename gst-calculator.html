GST Calculation Formulas

Understand the mathematical logic used to add or remove GST.

1. Adding GST (Tax Exclusive)

Used when the base amount does not include tax, and you want to compute the final price.

GST = Net Amount × (Rate / 100)
Gross = Net Amount + GST

2. Removing GST (Tax Inclusive)

Used when the total price includes tax, and you want to find the original pre-tax value.

Net = Gross Amount / (1 + (Rate / 100))
GST = Gross Amount − Net Amount
