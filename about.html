About FinCalc Tools

FinCalc Tools was built with a simple goal: to provide individuals, freelancers, and businesses with highly accurate, exceptionally fast, and completely private computing resources.

Our Core Values

We believe tools should be functional and clean. We do not require account creation, capture your variables, or plague your browser with popups. Everything runs smoothly and cleanly in-browser.

Privacy Focused

Your financial figures are your business. By utilizing local JavaScript computing models, your loan schedules, income taxes, and net take-home earnings are calculated in memory without ever hitting a server.

Our Technologies

FinCalc Tools leverages cutting-edge web hosting and database technology to guarantee 100% uptime:

  • Cloudflare Pages & Edge Network: Delivers pre-rendered HTML and Javascript assets globally in milliseconds.
  • Cloudflare Pages Functions, D1, and KV: Drives our custom URL Shortener and clicks log analytics dashboard without using bulk database queries.
  • Firebase Authentication & Security Rules: Authenticates profile management and account customization records securely.
