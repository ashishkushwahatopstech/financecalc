How Our Online Age Calculator Works

Calculating chronological age accurately requires accounting for leap years, varying month lengths (28, 30, and 31 days), and time zones. Our free age calculator online determines your exact age in years, months, and days from your birth date up to today or any custom chosen date.

In addition to exact age, this tool calculates lifetime milestones like total days and weeks lived, hours lived, day of week on your birthday, western astrological zodiac sign, and an accurate countdown to your next birthday celebration.
